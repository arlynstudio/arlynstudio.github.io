EcoCollect: smart waste management

My BSIT thesis project (SMART). It's a web app that helps a community in Poblacion, Sultan Naga Dimaporo manage waste collection, and it works on phones.

  • Residents create an account and pick their street, and their collection zone fills in automatically.
  • Email verification and password reset keep accounts secure.
  • Waste collectors get accounts through an administrator.
